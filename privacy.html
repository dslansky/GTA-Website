Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This website is run by the Greentree Acres bungalow colony community in Ferndale, NY. We collect only the information needed to run the site's features, we don't run ads, and we never sell or rent anyone's information. Here's the full picture.

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, phone, email, and message are delivered to the colony's email so we can respond. Submissions are kept for up to one year.
  • Shop orders: your name, email, and the items you order are stored so we can fulfill the order, and are shared with the vendor filling it. They aren't used for marketing.
  • Memories submissions: the name, caption, and photos or videos you share are published publicly on the Memories page. Only submit photos you have the right to share.
  • Push notifications: if you opt in to community updates, your browser gives us an anonymous delivery address for notifications. It isn't linked to your name or email, and you can unsubscribe anytime in your browser settings.

Information collected automatically

  • When you view or submit the contact form, we log basic technical details (IP address, browser type, country) to help prevent spam and abuse. View logs are deleted after 90 days; submission logs after one year.
  • Like nearly every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses as part of serving pages and protecting the site from attacks.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other sites. The only cookies we set are login cookies for the site administrators and for residents accessing the password-protected directory. Your browser also stores small preferences locally (like a dismissed banner or cached weather) that never leave your device.

The resident directory

The resident directory is password-protected and intended for Greentree Acres residents only. If you'd like your listing updated or removed, contact us and we'll take care of it.

Photos and videos

Community photos and videos on this site are shared by residents. If you or your child appears in a photo or video you'd like taken down, email us and we'll remove it promptly — no questions asked.

Services we rely on

These providers process data on our behalf to make the site work:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the website and stores submitted data.
  • Web3Forms — delivers contact-form messages to our email.
  • Google — page fonts, and a private spreadsheet used to manage shop orders.
  • YouTube — community videos, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (no tracking cookies until you play a video).
  • Hebcal & the National Weather Service — zmanim times and weather, fetched by your browser when you view those features.

Questions or removal requests

Email gtabungalows@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. If we ever make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
