Our Story

Rooted in Catskills History

Long before it became Greentree Acres, this land was part of the storied Borscht Belt — that beloved stretch of Sullivan County where New York's Jewish community built a world apart each summer. The property was once home to Julene's, a beloved bungalow colony that drew generations of families to the fresh mountain air and carefree days of Ferndale.

The Catskill Mountains were more than a vacation destination. They were a community, a culture, and a way of life — filled with shared meals, lakeside laughter, evening entertainment, and friendships that outlasted the season.

Today, Greentree Acres carries that tradition forward. The names and faces have changed, but the spirit hasn't: this is still a place where families come to breathe, reconnect, and make memories that last generations.
