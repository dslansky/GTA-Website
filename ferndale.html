About the Project

The Borscht Belt Historical Marker Project

The Borscht Belt Historical Marker Project dedicates historic markers throughout the Catskills to commemorate the heritage of the region's resort communities — the hotels, bungalow colonies, and social institutions that shaped a generation of American Jewish life.

With 15 markers already installed across Sullivan County — and five new dedications planned for 2026 — the project is building a permanent, publicly accessible record of a culture that transformed the Catskills into a world-famous summer destination.

Greentree Acres is proud to support their work. The colony is documented on the project's Ferndale page alongside the 60+ hotels and bungalow colonies that once called this hamlet home.

Ferndale's Heritage

Over 60 Establishments Called Ferndale Home

At its peak, Ferndale was home to dozens of hotels and bungalow colonies — each one a community unto itself. Greentree Acres, which stands on grounds once known as Julene's, is one of the few still operating today.
